    I build the foundation of cinematic worlds.

    From Rotoscoping and Paint to Matchmove, BodyTrack, Layout, and FX, my journey across the VFX pipeline shapes how I approach every shot: with precision, clarity, and storytelling.

    I started my career crafting clean mattes and seamless plates. I moved into Camera Tracking and BodyTrack, solving complex camera movement and performance data for AAA film and streaming projects.

    Today, I work in VFX Layout & Houdini FX, creating technically solid, production-ready shots that bridge animation, lighting, and final delivery.

    I also work with Unreal Engine for Virtual Production and Environment Design, building immersive real-time worlds and understanding modern real-time pipelines.

    Every department I've worked in sharpened one thing: Strong foundations create strong visuals.
